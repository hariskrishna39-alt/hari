My Journey

From sketchbooks to shipped products

How a habit of drawing everything turned into a career solving problems with pixels.

✏️
The beginning

A passion for drawing

Long before "UX" was a career plan, I was the kid filling notebook margins with layouts, posters, and imaginary app screens.

🎨
Getting started

Started with UI Design

My first real projects were purely visual — color, type, layout. I learned to make things look good before I learned why they worked.

🧭
The turning point

Learned UX

The "why" arrived with research and usability testing. Once I saw how much a bad flow costs a real user, I couldn't unsee it.

🏢
Going deep

Worked on enterprise products

Dashboards, internal tools, and workflows used by hundreds of employees daily — where clarity has measurable ROI.

🧩
Scaling up

Built design systems

Turned one-off screens into reusable, documented components so teams stopped rebuilding the same button five different ways.

💻
Broadening scope

Designed responsive websites

Marketing sites, storefronts, and content platforms designed to hold up from a 27-inch monitor to a phone in one hand.

📱
Going mobile

Worked on mobile applications

Native iOS and Android apps where every tap has to earn its place on a smaller, more distracted screen.

🎯
Today

Focused on solving user problems

Every project still starts the same way it did in that notebook — with a real problem worth drawing my way through.
